1. **Chronological Subset Non-Independence (`W1 ⊂ W3`):** `ToN-IoT W1` (`89,269`, Shards 22–23) is a strict chronological subset of `W3` (`191,738`, Shards 8–23); they are never pooled or treated as independent datasets.
2. **Severe Class-Imbalance Regime Boundary on `W3` (`53:1`):** `W3 X_val` is `98.15%` attack (`~2–6` benign rows/client/round) and `D_fl_train` is `97.93%` attack. Under `8`-step proportional replay and frozen `tau_val`, `drift_gated_replay` yields `0%` stationary FAR but `NS` parametric recovery/forgetting tests (`U-B4-05`). Whether minority-aware replay or dynamic threshold adaptation resolves `W3` remains **untested**.
3. **Single Pre-Onset Quorum Event (`W1 seed=202 R3`):** `3/10` clients triggered PSI simultaneously on clean round `R3` (`24` steps), identical across all `4` schedules × `2` gated arms (`+2.4` mean steps on `W1` drifted schedules; `W1` `stable_control` fleet FAR `4/100 = 4.0%`, well inside `<= 10%`). Together with `W1 seed=505 R6` (`recurring` `OFF` round, `1/30 = 3.3%`), this accounts for `100%` of non-drift adaptations.
4. **Borderline Step-Down Holm Clamping (`F3_W1`):** Rank-2 raw $p = 0.01669077 \times 3 = 0.05007231$ missed `0.05` by `7.23e-5`, clamping ranks 2–4 to `EXPLORATORY_RAW_ONLY`.
5. **Minor Historical Metadata Notes (Non-Blocking):** Frozen `v5.0` text rounded `StratifiedShuffleSplit(0.15)` by `1` row (`53,115/9,373` vs. verified `53,114/9,374` on `W1`; `125,252/22,103` vs. verified `125,251/22,104` on `W3`), and Gate 1's historical report (`GAP_1_DETERMINISM_PILOT_REPORT.md` §3) contained a transcription typo in the `pilot_manifest.json` hash (already documented in `GAP_1_POST_PILOT_AUDIT_REPORT.md` and `GAP_1_POST_CONFIRMATORY_AUDIT_REPORT.md`); all runtime manifests and Gate 2/audit reports carry the exact verified values.
6. **Untested Real-World Conditions:** Natural chronological drift, conditional concept drift ($P(y \mid x)$ shift), DP-enabled continual adaptation, active SecAgg, physical IoT gateway deployment, and cross-dataset transfer (`E5: HOLD`) remain unverified.
