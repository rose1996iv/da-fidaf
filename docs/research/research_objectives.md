# Research Objectives

!!! note "Alignment Caveat"
    The objectives below synthesize verified secondary project records (`README.md`, Phase 1–3B.2 specifications, and the two latest draft architecture diagrams). Exact numbering and wording must be cross-checked once the original approved PhD research proposal is provided (`GAP-01`).

1. **RO1 — Leak-Free Heterogeneous Federated IDS Pipeline:** Design and validate a reproducible, chronologically disciplined federated IDS pipeline for heterogeneous IoT traffic that prevents temporal, split, preprocessing, and duplicate-shard leakage.
2. **RO2 — Non-IID Resilience Across Heterogeneous IoT Gateways:** Quantify and mitigate performance degradation under label skew, quantity skew, feature skew, and combined heterogeneity across simulated IoT gateways.
3. **RO3 — Concept Drift Detection and Continual Adaptation:** Detect benign covariate shift and abrupt/gradual concept drift locally and globally, and evaluate continual adaptation mechanisms (such as replay-assisted recovery) without catastrophic forgetting.
4. **RO4 — Multi-Evidence Drift–Attack Attribution:** Distinguish legitimate benign concept drift from adversarial manipulation (label flipping, feature/data poisoning, backdoor triggers, Byzantine update corruption) using multi-evidence update analysis while controlling false attribution of benign-drifted clients.
5. **RO5 — Attribution-Guided, Risk-Aware Robust Aggregation:** Couple attribution decisions (`Benign Drift`, `Uncertain`, `Malicious`) with graduated aggregation actions (`Accept`, `Downweight`, `Hold`, `Quarantine`) and Byzantine-robust aggregation rules without excessive benign-client over-quarantine.
6. **RO6 — Privacy-Preserving & Cross-Dataset Generalization *(Planned / Not Yet Executed)*:** Evaluate formal privacy mechanisms (Differential Privacy and/or Secure Aggregation) and cross-dataset generalization across ToN-IoT, CICIoT2023, and Edge-IIoTset (E5, currently `HOLD`) once semantic ontology and proposal alignment are finalized.
